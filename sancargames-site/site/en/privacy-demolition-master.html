Privacy Policy

Demolition Master · Last updated: 14 November 2026

Demolition Master is an iPhone game published by SAMFA Gayrimenkul Yatırım ve Yönetim A.Ş. (Sancar Games). This policy explains what information the game uses and how.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The game has no account, sign-in or chat; it never asks for your name, email, contacts or location. The only data that reaches us is the usage statistics and crash reports described below, which are not matched to your identity.

Promo codes: if you enter a promo code in the game, the fact that the code has been used is recorded in our database on Google's Firebase service so the same code cannot be used twice. The record contains only a fingerprint of the code and the time of use; it contains nothing about you or your device.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, gold, machines, cosmetics, sound and vibration settings) is stored on your device. So you can continue on a new phone, a copy is kept in Apple's iCloud, in the space that belongs to your own Apple ID. We cannot see this copy. You can turn iCloud off in the iPhone Settings.

Ads

The game shows ads through Google AdMob: optional rewarded ads (for extra time or extra gold, for example) and an occasional full-screen interstitial ad. If you buy “No Ads” or a pack that includes it, interstitial ads are no longer shown.

The game asks for tracking permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you allow it, ads may be personalised to your interests; if you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalised. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

To show ads, prevent abuse and measure ads, Google may receive some technical information from the device: IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country/city), device type and operating system, ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and error/performance information and, only if you allow tracking, Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA). Google processes this information under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before ads; you can change your choice at any time under “Privacy options” in the game's Settings.

Statistics and crash reports

From version 1.0.1, to improve the game we use Google's Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics. Analytics records events that show how the game is played, such as the first launch, session length, a level starting, being completed or running out of time, a rewarded ad being watched and a purchase being made. When the game crashes or an error occurs, Crashlytics sends a report with the device model, iOS version and technical details of the error.

This data is grouped by a random installation ID that Firebase creates for the app. It is not linked to your name, your Apple ID or your advertising identifier (IDFA), it is not used for tracking or ad personalisation, and we only review it in aggregate to improve the game. Deleting the app also deletes this installation ID. How Google processes this data: firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

Permissions and Apple services

Tracking permission: described under “Ads” above.

Game Center: the game may ask you to sign in to Apple Game Center. Sign-in is handled entirely by Apple; your Game Center account details are not shared with us.

Notifications: if you allow them, the game schedules reminders on your device. Reminders are created on the iPhone itself; no information is sent to any server for this. You can turn them off in iPhone Settings → Notifications.

The game does not ask for camera, microphone, photo, contacts or location permission.

Purchases and external links

The game has in-app purchases. Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details; the game only learns what you bought so it can unlock it.

Links in the game may open the App Store, the support page on this website, our YouTube and Instagram pages or the iPhone share sheet; those services are subject to their own privacy rules.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent and have a question or concern, write to us.

Your rights

We keep no record that identifies you, so we hold no personal data of yours that we could show or delete. You can remove the game data on your device by deleting the app. For questions and requests under GDPR, KVKK or similar laws, write to us.

Usage statistics and crash reports are not matched to your identity, and deleting the app resets the installation ID.

Changes

If we change this policy, we publish the current version and the new date on this page.
