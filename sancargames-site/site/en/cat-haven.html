Cat Haven: Hide and Seek

Street cats are hiding in the park, at the market, by the harbor and on the rooftops. Spot their tails, sneak up, catch them and bring every one of them home.

Download on theApp StoreFree · iPhone and iPad · 20 languages

How to play

See that tail?

Cats hide behind bushes, market stalls, boats and chimneys, and often only the tip of a tail peeks out. Look closely and move quietly.

30 levels, 3 difficulties

From the neighborhood park to a snowy winter, from the train station to the fair, from a night train to a foggy harbor: easy, hard and master courses.

20 cats, 20 personalities

Sleepy Marmalade, Honey who creeps closer, Smokey who turns almost invisible, zigzagging Ginger. Collect stars to unlock new cats.
