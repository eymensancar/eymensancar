Lantern Detective

I'm stuck. What should I do?

Tap the 💡 button in the room to see that room's hint. When answering a case's questions, check your 📓 Detective Notebook: every answer comes from putting the clues together.

There's no sound.

Make sure the phone isn't in silent mode and the volume is up. “Narration” must be on in Settings.

Did I lose my progress?

Progress is stored on the phone. Deleting the app also deletes the progress.
