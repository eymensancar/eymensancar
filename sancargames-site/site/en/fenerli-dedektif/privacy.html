Privacy Policy

Lantern Detective · Last updated: November 2026

Lantern Detective is developed by SAMFA Gayrimenkul Yatırım ve Yönetim A.Ş. (Sancar Games). The game is designed for children, and its privacy setup follows from that.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. There is no account or login, and the game does not use your name, email, location, photos or microphone. Game progress (unlocked rooms, stars, settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us. The game contains no analytics tools.

Ads

The game is free; ads are shown by Google AdMob. We mark ad requests as child-directed (“tag for child-directed treatment” and “tag for under age of consent” are on), allow only ads suitable for general audiences, and ads are not personalized. The app does not request Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and does not access the advertising identifier (IDFA). To show an ad and prevent abuse, Google may process limited technical information such as device type, operating system version and approximate country; details: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Purchases and links

The game has no in-app purchases. Links that leave the game are behind a parental gate (a question for grown-ups).

Narration

Turkish and English narration audio is bundled in the app; other languages use the device's built-in text-to-speech. Nothing is recorded.

Children's privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents with questions can contact us.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page.
