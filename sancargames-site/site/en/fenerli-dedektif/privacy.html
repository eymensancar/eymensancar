Privacy Policy

Lantern Detective · Last updated: 14 November 2026

Lantern Detective is an iPhone game published by SAMFA Gayrimenkul Yatırım ve Yönetim A.Ş. (Sancar Games). This policy explains what information the game uses and how.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information. The game has no account, sign-in or chat; it never asks for your name, email, contacts or location. The game sends us no data at all.

What is stored on your device

Your game progress (unlocked rooms, stars and settings) is stored only on your device and is not sent to us.

Ads

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: an optional rewarded ad for a hint and an occasional full-screen interstitial ad.

Because the game is designed for children, we mark ad requests as child-directed (“tag for child-directed treatment” and “tag for under age of consent” are on) and allow only ads suitable for a general audience. Ads are not personalised. The game does not ask for Apple's app tracking permission and does not track you across apps or websites.

To show ads, prevent abuse and measure ads, Google may receive some technical information from the device: IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country/city), device type and operating system, ad interactions (impressions, clicks) and error/performance information. Google processes this information under its own privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Statistics and crash reports

The game has no tool that sends statistics, analytics or crash reports.

Permissions and Apple services

The game asks for no permissions: it never asks for tracking, notification, camera, microphone, photo, contacts or location permission.

Narration: Turkish and English narration voices are included in the app; other languages use the device's own speech feature. No audio is recorded.

Purchases and external links

The game has no purchases.

Links that leave the game (the help and privacy pages on this website) are behind a parental gate (a question adults can answer).

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. If you are a parent and have a question or concern, write to us.

The game is designed for children aged 7–11 and their families; privacy and ad settings were chosen accordingly.

Your rights

We keep no record that identifies you, so we hold no personal data of yours that we could show or delete. You can remove the game data on your device by deleting the app. For questions and requests under GDPR, KVKK or similar laws, write to us.

Changes

If we change this policy, we publish the current version and the new date on this page.
