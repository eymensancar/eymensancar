Privacy Policy

Number Path · Last updated: 8 November 2026

This policy explains what information is kept where while you play Number Path (the “App”).

No account, no personal data

The App does not ask you to create an account. Your name, email, contacts, location or any other personal information are never collected.

What is stored on your device

Your progress (levels, stars, best times, daily puzzle streak, hints and settings) is stored only on your device.

Anonymous usage statistics

To learn which levels are too hard and whether players come back, the App sends anonymous, aggregated counts, for example “level 12 started”, “level 12 finished in 48 seconds”, “daily puzzle solved”, “a hint was used”. Each batch carries only the app version, the app language and the date the App was installed. There is no identifier of any kind (no advertising ID, no device ID, no account), so the counts cannot be linked to you or to your device, and they are not used for tracking or advertising. They are stored in a spreadsheet only we can read and are used only to improve the game.

Game Center

The App may ask you to sign in to Apple Game Center for achievements and leaderboards. Sign-in, scores and achievements are handled entirely by Apple; your Game Center account details are not shared with us.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the App schedules reminders (for example for the daily puzzle) on your device. They are created on the iPhone itself; nothing is sent to a server. You can turn them off in the App's settings or in iPhone Settings.

Ads

When you have no hints left, the App can show an optional promotion for our other game. It is drawn by the App itself; no ad network is used and no data is collected.

Children

We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

If we update this policy, we will change the date on this page. Questions: support@sancargames.com
