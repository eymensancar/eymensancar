Privacy Policy

Cat Haven (Kedi Yuvası) · Last updated: 14 November 2026

Cat Haven is an iPhone game published by SAMFA Gayrimenkul Yatırım ve Yönetim A.Ş. (Sancar Games). This policy explains what information the game uses and how.

Information we collect

None. The game has no accounts, no login, no chat and never asks for personal information. Your progress (stars, unlocked cats, settings) is stored only on your device and is never sent to us.

Ads

The game is free and shows ads through Google AdMob: an optional “watch an ad to continue” after losing a level, and an occasional full-screen ad between levels. Ad requests are tagged as child-directed, limited to content suitable for general audiences, and are not personalized. The game does not ask for Apple's App Tracking Transparency permission and does not track you across apps or websites.

To show ads, Google may receive some technical information from the device: approximate location derived from the IP address (country/city), device type and operating system, ad interactions in the app (ad shown, ad tapped) and crash/performance information. This information is subject to Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google's consent form is shown before ads; you can change your choice at any time with “Privacy options” in the game's Settings.

Children

The game is for players of all ages. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The ad settings are configured to be appropriate for children.

Internet connection

The game can be played offline; the internet is used only for ads.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will publish the updated version on this page.
