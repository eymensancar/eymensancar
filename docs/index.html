The demolition game iPhone was waiting for

If you have ever watched a controlled demolition video and wanted to press the button yourself, Demolition Master was made for you. It is a building demolition game where every structure behaves like the real thing: take out the right columns and the floors above come crashing down under their own weight.

This is not a mindless smash-everything game. It is part puzzle, part construction-site simulator: read the building, pick the machine that matches the material, and plan the collapse. From a wooden shed to steel-frame skyscrapers, from factory districts to whole city blocks waiting for urban renewal, every level asks you to think like a real demolition engineer.

Swing a crane-mounted wrecking ball, cut steel with a torch, crack concrete with a breaker, then finish the job with dynamite and C4. The physics are satisfying, the chain reactions are huge, and you can play it all offline, for free.

Questions people ask

Is there a good building demolition game for iPhone?

Yes. Demolition Master is a free iPhone game built entirely around demolishing buildings, with realistic structural collapse, seven different machines and explosives.

Is there a game where you knock down buildings for urban renewal?

In Demolition Master you clear whole sites: rows of houses, factories, office plazas and malls. Each site is a puzzle about where to cut so everything comes down.

Can I blow up skyscrapers with dynamite and C4?

Yes. Plant dynamite on the supporting columns, use C4 controlled demolition on reinforced concrete and set off gas tanks for chain explosions.

Does it work without internet?

Yes, the whole game plays offline. Your progress is saved on your iPhone and backed up to your iCloud.

Is it free?

Yes, Demolition Master is free to download and play. Optional in-app purchases and optional rewarded ads are available.
